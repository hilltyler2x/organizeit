OrganizeIt: sort your camera roll automatically

OrganizeIt connects to your phone's photo library and sorts every photo and video into a clear, specific category on its own. Business expense screenshots (receipts, invoices, order confirmations, payment screens) go to their own section. Product photos are sorted by exact item type, like Bags, Wallets, Hats or Sneakers. It shows the storage each category uses, flags duplicates, and never uses an Other, Misc or Uncategorized folder.

Get OrganizeIt on Google Play. The iPhone app is coming to the App Store. The first 100 photos are sorted free.
