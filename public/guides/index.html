OrganizeIt

OrganizeIt guides

OrganizeIt is a photo organizer app for iPhone and Android that sorts your whole camera roll into specific categories on its own. It keeps business expense screenshots (receipts, invoices, order confirmations, payment screens) in their own section, sorts product photos by exact item type, shows the storage each category uses, and never puts anything in an Other, Misc or Uncategorized folder.
