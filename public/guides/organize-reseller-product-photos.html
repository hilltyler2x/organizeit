How do resellers organize product photos on their phone?

Most resellers shoot dozens of photos per item, and the camera roll quickly fills with thousands of pictures of bags, shoes and clothing. OrganizeIt sorts those product photos by exact item type (Bags, Wallets, Card Holders, Hats, Beanies, Shirts, Shorts, Pants, Belts, Fragrance, Sneakers and so on), creates new categories as your inventory changes, and shows how much storage each category uses so you can clean up sold items in bulk.

Why listing apps don't solve it

Crosslisting tools like Vendoo, List Perfectly and Flyp handle listings once you pick the photos. They don't sort the camera roll itself. Your phone still holds every shot of every item, mixed in with personal photos and receipts.

A workflow that keeps the camera roll clean

  1. Shoot your items as usual.
  2. OrganizeIt files the photos by item type in the background.
  3. Open a category (say Bags) to find photos when you list.
  4. After items sell, open the category, select the photos and delete them in bulk to get the storage back.
  5. Use the Duplicates list to delete near-identical retakes and keep the best shot.

Questions

Does it work with eBay or Poshmark?
It doesn't post listings. It organizes the photos on your phone, and you can put any category into a Photos album to pick from when you list in your usual app.
What if I sell something new, like sunglasses?
OrganizeIt creates a new Sunglasses category. It never dumps photos in Other or Misc.
