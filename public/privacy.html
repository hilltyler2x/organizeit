OrganizeIt privacy policy

OrganizeIt sorts the photos and videos on your phone into categories. This page explains what leaves your phone and why.

Your photos stay on your phone

OrganizeIt reads your photo library on the phone itself. Your photos and videos are not uploaded or stored anywhere else. The categories, labels and your corrections are kept on your phone.

What is sent to be sorted

To sort a photo, OrganizeIt sends a small, low-resolution preview of it (for a video, six still frames) together with its file name and size to the OrganizeIt server. The server passes it to Anthropic's Claude AI to name the category and sends the answer back. The server does not keep the preview. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play; OrganizeIt never sees your card. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for OrganizeIt, receives an anonymous id for your phone and your purchase receipts so the app knows you are subscribed. The OrganizeIt server keeps that id with a count of photos sorted each month, to apply the free preview and monthly allowances.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account or email required.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your phone. To have the photo counts tied to your phone's id removed from the server, contact us through the support link on OrganizeIt's App Store or Google Play page.
