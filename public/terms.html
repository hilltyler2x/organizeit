OrganizeIt terms of use

On iPhone, OrganizeIt is licensed under Apple's standard licensed application end user license agreement. On Android, Google Play's terms of service apply. The points below explain the subscription.

Free preview

Each phone can have its first 100 photos sorted free, without a subscription.

Subscription

OrganizeIt Monthly sorts your library and keeps sorting new photos as they arrive. New subscribers can get a discounted first month; the price is shown before you buy. After that the subscription renews every month at the regular price shown in the app until you cancel.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

The discounted first month includes up to 2,000 photos. Any photos beyond that are sorted once the subscription renews at the regular price.

Cancelling

Manage or cancel the subscription any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions (iPhone) or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions (Android). Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Sorting

Categories are chosen by AI and can be wrong; photos it is unsure about are shown to you to check. OrganizeIt only deletes photos when you ask it to.
